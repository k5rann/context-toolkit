# The Complete Human Writing Framework — Reference

Saved from user-provided Google Doc. Used as source material for chain prompt design.

## Key Detection Signals (distilled for prompt engineering)

### Level 1 — Banned Vocabulary
Core kills: serves as, underscore, foster, enhance, delve, navigate, tapestry, nuanced,
multifaceted, pivotal, robust, leverage (verb), innovative, transformative, groundbreaking,
revolutionary, holistic, comprehensive, multidisciplinary, empower, stakeholder, impactful,
"it is worth noting", furthermore, moreover, additionally, "in addition",
"not just X but also Y", "despite X, Y" (filler concession), "of course", "at the end of the day",
ultimately, "in conclusion", "to summarise", "key takeaways", absolutely, certainly,
"great question", "moving forward", "going forward", "at this juncture", "one must",
"it behoves", "one cannot overstate".

Inflated → honest: "due to the fact that"→"because", "at this point in time"→"now",
"has the ability to"→"can", "in order to"→"to", "in the event that"→"if",
"with regard to"→"about", "on a daily basis"→"daily", "make a decision"→"decide".

### Level 2 — Sentence
- Three-pass: who acts? what specifically happened? is this removable?
- Length variation is THE signal AI never follows
- Deliberate fragments permitted: "Not good enough." "Three weeks, no response."
- Kill -ing constructions that open sentences
- Contractions everywhere (it's, don't, can't, there's, we've, they're)
- "I don't know" is human; "it remains to be seen" is AI

### Level 3 — Paragraph
- Must begin with claim, end somewhere the opening couldn't predict
- Show events not categories: "lost its two most senior engineers" not "faced challenges"
- Name costs: what was sacrificed, what failed first

### Level 4 — Opening
- Start in the middle, not with framing
- Five types: specific scene, contradiction, reframing fact, blunt claim, unanswerable question
- Second-paragraph retreat is a top AI signal (context after strong opening)

### Level 5 — Structure
- Remove bullet points where prose serves better
- Vary paragraph length deliberately (1 sentence, then 8, then 2)
- Safety sandwich removal (positive-negative-positive pattern)
- Lists shouldn't all be exactly 5 or 10 items

### Level 6 — Tone
- Remove uniform enthusiasm
- Vary weight and speed to match content
- Humour from observation not construction

### Level 7 — Specificity
- Physical/sensory grounding even in abstract topics
- Temporal specificity (show duration)
- Unverifiable sources → flag or remove

### Level 8 — Register
- Write for specific reader, not universal
- Identify implied audience from vocabulary, stakes, pronouns

### Level 9 — Counter-argument
- Genuine engagement costs certainty
- "Both perspectives have merit" is the AI signal
- State objection at full strength, bring evidence, admit limits

### Level 10 — Voice
- Take positions specific enough to be wrong
- Self-interruption: "But that assumes they knew, and I'm not sure they did"
- Genuine uncertainty with named gaps

### Level 11 — Macro-shape
- Pacing variation: slow for hard ideas, fast for clear conclusions
- Single-sentence paragraphs for weight
- Don't give every section identical depth

### Level 12 — Adversarial re-read
- Inhabited-writing test: does a specific mind feel present?
- Weakest claim identification
- Over-correction warning: specificity as performance
