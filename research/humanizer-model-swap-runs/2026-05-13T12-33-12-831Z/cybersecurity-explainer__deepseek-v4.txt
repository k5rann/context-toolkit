Cybersecurity is a major concern. Organizations face a rapidly evolving threat landscape. Malicious actors develop new methods to exploit vulnerabilities - sophisticated phishing campaigns, ransomware attacks on critical infrastructure. A multifaceted approach is needed. Technical safeguards matter. So does employee awareness.

Companies need robust solutions. Protect sensitive information. Monitor unusual activity. Respond quickly when incidents happen. Technical tools alone aren't enough. One employee clicking the wrong link creates significant exposure. Training, clear policies, regular testing build resilience.

AI-powered threats are emerging. Proactive defense is critical now. Organizations that don't keep pace risk financial loss, reputational damage, operational disruption.
