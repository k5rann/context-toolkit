Figure 1 illustrates the functioning of ADAPT within an actual financial organization. The system takes in real-time transaction information, refines it through steps like cleaning, normalization, and feature engineering, and then implements techniques to identify anomalies. The part of the system called Isolation Forest spots atypical actions specific to each customer, whereas the layer based on graphs uncovers dubious connections among accounts and transactions. The ultimate score for fraud risk decides if a transaction receives approval.
