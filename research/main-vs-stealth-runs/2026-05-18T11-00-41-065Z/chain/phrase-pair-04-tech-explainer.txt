A single employee clicking on a bad link can cause big problems, even with strong security systems in place. This shows why training, clear policies, and regular tests are so important for building strong defenses. Cyber threats are changing quickly, from clever phishing to ransomware attacks on critical systems. Hackers keep finding new ways to break in and steal sensitive data, making it essential for companies to use advanced tools that spot unusual activity and react fast. With AI threats growing, companies need proactive strategies to protect their operations and reputation. Addressing both tech and human factors is key. Effective protection needs a mix of tech solutions and employee awareness, ultimately ensuring business stability and trust. Note: Regular training sessions are crucial.
