The NovaLite Pro Smart Bottle it reminds you to drink water and controls temperature. Because of special sensors inside it connects with a mobile app so you can see how much water you consumed. This is not only a bottle but also health tool. It is for athletes and also for busy professionals. Made from good material the design is ergonomic and nice looking. Real time data shows you about your hydration so you can improve your habits. The bottle tracks water intake daily and gives insights. I think it is decent product for staying healthy. By the way the app sends alerts personal to you. It works simple and you dont need to learn complicated things. Very useful. It gives you info that helps you decide when to drink more. The bottle keeps water cold or hot for long time. Design is fine and tough.
