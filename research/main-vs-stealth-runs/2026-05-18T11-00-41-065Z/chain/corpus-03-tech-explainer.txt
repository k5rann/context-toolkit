In today's digital world, a single employee clicking a bad link can expose the company, even with advanced security. Malicious actors are always changing their methods to find weak spots. Phishing and ransomware attacks on important systems are increasing, and AI-powered threats add to the worry. Firms must set up strong systems to watch network activity, catch problems in real-time, and act quickly when something happens. It's not a matter of if but when they will face an attack. Training, clear guidelines, and regular tests are key to building a resilient company. As threats get more complex, both technical defenses and human awareness are crucial to protect operations and reputation. By the way, AI threats are a growing concern for many businesses.
