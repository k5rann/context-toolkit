Look, cybersecurity is a huge problem now. Companies across every industry are just getting hit all the time. Bad guys keep finding new ways to break in and steal stuff, from tricky phishing emails to ransomware attacks that shut down whole systems. It just keeps shifting, so you need to set up a few different things to protect yourself. That means technical tools, but also thinking about the people.

To protect against all this, companies have to use tools that watch their network, catch strange things in real-time, and react quickly when something bad happens. But tools aren't enough. One employee clicking a bad link can open up a huge hole, no matter how good your security systems are. So training programs, clear rules, and regular testing all play a big part in keeping a company safe. As AI attacks keep coming, you just have to get ahead of it. It's how you stay online and don't look like an idiot.
