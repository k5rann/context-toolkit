Artificial Intelligence is one of the most revolutionary technologies of the 21st century in today's fast-changing digital world. AI systems are changing the way business, educational institutions and individuals make decisions by utilising complex algorithms and large amounts of data. Moreover, machine learning's application in daily business processes has opened up new horizons for increased productivity, optimized workflows and improved customer satisfaction in almost all areas of the global economy.

But this technological revolution has its problems. With the growing speed at which AI technology is developing, crucial ethical issues have recently entered the mainstream of public discussion, including privacy, accountability, and responsible AI use. A strong collaboration between stakeholders is essential to create adequate frameworks that allow these powerful tools to be developed and used in a way that is focused on improving human well-being and encouraging innovation. As AI continues to advance, the key to its future lies in how well humanity can manage its growth responsibly, transparently and ethically.
